Stage: motion. Produce a simplified color-coded dummy cycle, not a finished character.
Use the design reference only for proportions and foot/shoe volume; use the selected motion reference and reviewed keys for pose and phase order.
For articulated characters, use distinct flat diagnostic colors for head, torso and anatomical left/right limbs. For non-articulated effects, use simple moving color masses instead; do not invent body parts. Keep each limb's color across screen-side changes. Omit final skin tones, costume details, hair strands, shading and highlights.
Show contact, loading/down, passing under the pelvis and release/swing on both sides where appropriate to the anatomy. Preserve limb lengths and footwear proportions, including knee, ankle, heel and toe readability under justified occlusion/foreshortening.
Express the requested effort through posture, lean, stride, weight transfer and timing. Coordinate head/chest/pelvis with opposite arm swing and shoulder/hip counter-rotation. For humanlike running show landing compression, push-off and flight; walking must not acquire a flight phase.
In-place motion may have planned sway, bounce and lean; irregular strip spacing is not intentional travel. A stance foot moves backward relative to the body. Do not pin every foot or head or freeze the torso. Do not replace missing mechanics with faster playback.
Keep the approved key poses and diagnostic proportions readable at target size. This stage must pass motion review before flat character color or finishing.
